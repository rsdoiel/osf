osf 0.0.10

Modernization to the workspace standard. Go 1.27.3 is the minimum version. The three programs (fadein2osf, osf2txt, txt2osf) use the standard library flag package instead of github.com/caltechlibrary/cli, with -h, -l and -v beside the long spellings.
